Our Story

Familiar, yet unexpected.

Where it began

Born in Johor.

Best Tarik & Co was established in Johor, Malaysia, with a vision to bring a fresh and creative twist to the traditional Malaysian teh tarik. By combining familiar local flavours with unique ingredients, the brand introduces a new way to enjoy a beloved Malaysian classic.

Why taufufah

A drink and a dessert, in one cup.

Teh tarik with taufufah was made to be familiar yet unexpected. The rich, comforting taste of traditional teh tarik meets the soft, silky texture of soy pudding: a local beverage and a traditional dessert, together.

From a small pop-up

The first stall.

Every brand has a beginning. Best Tarik & Co started with pop-ups on the streets of Johor, introducing the drink to the local community one cup at a time.
